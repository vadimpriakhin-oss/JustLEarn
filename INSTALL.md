# Installing JustLEarn as a Native App

JustLEarn is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. You can install it directly from your browser — no App Store or Google Play account required.

---

## 📱 iOS (iPhone / iPad) — via Safari

> Safari is required. Chrome and other iOS browsers do not support PWA installation.

1. Open **Safari** and navigate to the JustLEarn URL.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down in the share sheet and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Edit the name if desired, then tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.
5. The JustLEarn icon will appear on your Home Screen.

Tap the icon to launch the app in full-screen, standalone mode.

---

## 🤖 Android — via Chrome

1. Open **Chrome** and navigate to the JustLEarn URL.
2. Chrome will automatically show an **"Add JustLEarn to Home screen"** banner at the bottom of the screen.  
   — *If the banner does not appear*, tap the **⋮ (three-dot menu)** in the top-right corner and select **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
3. Tap **"Install"** in the prompt that appears.
4. The JustLEarn icon will appear on your Home Screen / App Drawer.

Tap the icon to launch the app in standalone mode.

---

## ✈️ Offline support

Once installed (or after the first visit), JustLEarn works fully offline. All quiz content is cached automatically by the Service Worker.

---

## 🔄 Updating the app

The Service Worker checks for updates in the background on every launch. When a new version is available it will be applied automatically the next time you open the app.
